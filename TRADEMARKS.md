## Using your own logo

This matters more here than in the other clients, because in this one the mascot **is** the
artwork on screen rather than an icon in the corner. Putting your own logo in is the point of
the thing: set `PicturePath` in `logo.json` and pick **Mine, from the file** off the tray menu,
and none of the above is involved at all.

If you fork this into your own app, take the code, keep the code, ship the code — but delete
the three files above and put your own pictures and icon in their place, and do not use the
name in a way that suggests MeddlingIdiot published, endorsed or supports what you built.

Referring to the project by name — "built on the Greenlight SDK", "a fork of LogoClient" — is
fine, and always was.
